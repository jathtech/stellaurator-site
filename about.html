What is StellAurator?

StellAurator is audiobook recording and production software for Windows. Authors and narrators use it to record an audiobook from the manuscript, fix single lines, add music and export chapters in the format Audible’s ACX requires, in one program. Every recording is stored on the line of the manuscript it belongs to.

What StellAurator does

A book goes from manuscript to finished audiobook files in one window, and your progress is saved as you go.

  • Bring in your manuscript: a Word file, a PDF, an EPUB or plain text. Chapters are found at their headings and each line gets a speaker, which you review.
  • Cast it: each character gets a voice and a portrait, and you invite actors for any part.
  • Record: read from the teleprompter at your pace; each recording is filed under its line.
  • Clean up: fixes are suggested for each recording and applied as layers you can undo; the original is never modified.
  • Score: place music beds and cues on the chapter’s timeline.
  • Finish: Diagnose, Plan, Apply, Check, then Export, over a chapter or the whole book.
  • Listen and save: proof listen line by line, mark the pickups and export the finished files.

Every part is described on the studio page, and shown in the demos.

What StellAurator does not do

StellAurator does not:

  • Run on a Mac or a phone yet.
  • Import RTF or older .doc manuscripts, or read a PDF that is only pictures of pages (a scan).
  • Distribute or publish your audiobook: you upload the files to ACX or another store yourself.
  • Make a computer-voiced book that ACX accepts: ACX requires human narration unless it has authorized otherwise.
  • Remove room echo, or a sound under your voice, after recording.
  • Generate opening and closing credits, or pick the retail sample for you.
  • Ship music or sound effects: you bring your own or generate them through a service you connect.
