# Where the product images in the guides come from

Where the pictures come from, in the order the owner set on 2026-10-28:

1. the maker's own press pictures (a press room, a media kit, press-release photo files);
2. pictures whose copyright has been waived (CC0, public domain), from Wikimedia Commons;
3. a drawing that says it is a drawing (mic-*.svg, interface.svg, blanket.svg, headphones.svg, panel.svg, inline-preamp.svg, reflection-filter.svg, bass-trap.svg).

A card whose maker offers no press picture of that model keeps the picture from the maker's own website until the maker is asked; its row says so. Nothing here comes from a retailer or from Amazon (docs/AMAZON_IMAGES.md). Every picture is shown as published, scaled in proportion onto a white square (340 px, 170 px on the page) by tools/guide_tile.py. The research behind each choice, and the makers' terms, are in docs/GUIDE_PRESS_KITS.md and docs/guide-press-kits.json.
