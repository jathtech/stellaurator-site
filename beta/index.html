Open beta

The full studio, free until the beta ends

StellAurator is in open beta. A beta key gives a new account the full studio, export included, free until the beta ends, for people who will tell us what breaks and what they would change. No card is asked for, and nothing is charged. While the beta runs, a plan is $12 a month or $90 a year instead of the regular $29 and $290, kept while you stay subscribed; see pricing.

100 keys

One key per account, for new accounts. When the keys are gone, the free 7-day trial and the beta prices are still open to everyone.

When the trial ends, your books stay on your computer and still open and play. Recording, generating and editing wait for a plan.

Get my beta key

While you hold a beta pass, the app sends us basic usage counts when it starts, every 15 minutes and when it closes: how long it was open and which features were used, never your text, audio or names. What a report holds.

Record 20,000 words of your own book during the beta and your first year is free. We count the words of the lines you record; the app reports the count, never the text.

How it works

Three steps to your key

  1. Create your accountPress Get my beta key, then Create account. Use an email address and a password.
  2. Confirm your emailOpen the confirmation email from StellAurator and press the link in it. Then go back to the beta page.
  3. Claim your key and downloadPress Claim my beta key. Your key appears on the page and the pass is added to your account. Download the app and sign in with the same email address.

The rules

What the beta includes

The full studio

Everything, export included, on up to three of your computers. The free trial leaves export out; a beta key does not.

Nothing is charged

No card is asked for. The pass lasts until the beta ends. It does not turn into a paid plan, and nothing is charged when it ends.

Your files stay on your computer

Your projects and recordings stay on your computer. When the beta ends, every book still opens and plays; subscribe to keep recording and exporting.
