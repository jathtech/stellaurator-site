What you get

Three things the studio does

Your reading, filed under its line

Each line of your book has its own row. When you record a line, the take goes on that row and stays there, with every other take of that line, until you delete one. To fix a sentence, you find its line and read it again. You never scrub through an hour of audio looking for the good take.

Clicks and breaths, marked under each take

A fix for each one is listed under the take. You hear it before and after, then keep it or not. Before you upload, each chapter is measured against ACX’s requirements and gets a pass or a fail.

Your book stays on your computer

Your account holds your email address and your plan. When a plan ends, every book still opens and plays, and nothing is deleted.

Why I built StellAurator

I couldn’t find audiobook software made for authors, so I built it

I’m an author. When I started working on the audiobook for my novel, I realized pretty quickly that none of the software I was finding was actually made for authors. Everything was designed for recording music or podcasts. I wanted something where I could work directly from my manuscript, record my lines, and have everything organized without having to constantly switch between programs. I couldn’t find what I wanted, so I built it myself. That’s how StellAurator came about. It’s the software I built to make my own audiobooks.

J.T. Michaelstar

Questions

Three questions

Does StellAurator run on a Mac?

Not yet; it runs on 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. A Mac version is coming. Leave your email to hear when it is ready.

Does StellAurator use AI voices?

It records your voice. A character can also be given a computer voice, and those are described on the voices page.

What does it cost after the seven free days?

No card is taken, and nothing is charged. When the trial ends, your books stay on your computer and still open and play. Recording, generating and editing wait for a plan. During the open beta a plan is $12 a month or $90 a year, and you keep that price while you stay subscribed. The regular prices are $29 a month and $290 a year. The plans, on the pricing page.
