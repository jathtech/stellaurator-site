The workflow

From manuscript to finished audiobook.

Stop at any step and come back later. Editing is non-destructive: every take you generate or record is kept until you delete it.

  1. ImportBring in your manuscript and split it into chapters and lines.
  2. CastAssign a voice to each character. Optional AI help identifies who is speaking.
  3. CollaborateSend parts to other performers. Their takes come back into the book, under a signed release.
  4. PerformAct every part out against the teleprompter, at a pace you set.
  5. ConvertOne pass puts each line into its character’s voice, keeping your delivery.
  6. ProduceReorder, retime and level the audio.
  7. ScoreAuto Score finds the beats; lay music and sound effects under the lines.
  8. ExportACX chapter MP3s, a chaptered M4B, or both.

Inside the studio

Everything happens in one window.

Cast the book

Cast every character

The cast panel lists every speaker the importer found. Each one gets a color, a voice from whichever provider you have connected, and a face from a pool of 445 portraits.

Open Character Tags: click a character then click a portrait, drag one onto a slot, or let Best fit auto assign do the whole cast at once. Anyone still uncast is flagged, so you know what is left before you generate anything.

  • 445 portraits across seven galleries — sci-fi crew, deep space, fantasy folk, neo-noir city, masquerade couples, every age, and more
  • Best fit auto assign, or assign at random
  • Keep a cast’s faces when you switch themes, or let them restyle
  • Per-character voice, color, gender and pronouns
  • Aliases, so “Okoye” and “Captain Okoye” are one person
  • UNCAST warnings before you spend a single credit

Perform every character

You play every part

Your voice does not have to sound like the character, and it does not have to sound like more than one person. Open the teleprompter and act the scene out, every part, back to back. The current line sits in a focus band; the rest of the chapter fades above and below it, at a pace you set.

Then run the actor pass: speech-to-speech conversion. Every line comes back in the voice of the character who was speaking it, with your timing, your emphasis and your pauses.

  • Count-in before recording starts
  • Adjustable pace, text size and focus band
  • Live passthrough monitoring while you read
  • One click saves the newest raw take

Bring the recordings you already made

A chapter you recorded somewhere else becomes a chapter of takes here. Drop the file in, say which chapter it is, and the studio cuts it into your lines — on the pauses, which is free and stays on your computer, or on the words, with a transcription service you connect.

Your file is never changed and never re-mastered; the studio only divides it. Where the recording and the manuscript disagree, you choose, line by line.

  • mp3, wav, flac, m4a, m4b and ogg
  • Split on the pauses — free, and nothing leaves your computer

Direct and edit every take

Keep every take, choose between them

Each line has a clip pool. Generate a take, record another, convert a third: every one stays in the pool with its waveform and length, and you lock in the one you want.

Version columns A and B let you build two complete renditions of the same book at once and export either one, so you can try a different narrator without losing the first pass.

  • Unlimited alternate takes per line
  • Lock in the take you want; the rest stay available
  • Version columns A, B, and as many more as you add
  • Flag filters: TTS, voice-converted, raw, unlocked, accepted

Master and export

Your recordings, cleaned up as they are saved

Every recording is cleaned up on its way in. Low rumble is removed, harsh S sounds are softened, and loudness is evened out. Three sliders warm, sharpen or brighten the tone. Auto-level normalizes every take to the same peak level without changing how you said it.

  • Removes rumble, softens harsh S sounds, evens out loudness
  • Three tone sliders: warmth, presence, air
  • Auto-level fixes quiet takes and too-loud ones
  • Pronunciation guide for names the voices get wrong

Auto Score your book

Auto Score reads each chapter and finds the beats: where a scene turns, where the tension peaks, where music belongs. For each one it writes a cue, with a mood and one sentence saying why, and ties the cue to the words it belongs to, so it stays in the right place when you edit the chapter.

Lay your own music and sound effects under the lines: a bed runs under a scene, a sting or an effect sits between two lines, and every cue is a marker on the timeline. Print the cue sheet for a composer.

  • Beds, swells and stings, with a mood and an intensity
  • Every cue is tied to the words you quote, not to a timecode
  • Your own music and effects, as tracks under the lines
  • Cue markers on the chapter timeline
  • A printable cue sheet

Export to the industry standard

Export ACX-standard chapter MP3s, a single chaptered M4B with cover art, or both in one run. You choose which takes are used: your locked-in selections, the alternates, or an entire version column.

The gaps between lines are measured and corrected on the way out. Silence is cut or extended so that the pause after a sentence, after a paragraph and after a section break is exactly the length you set.

  • ACX: one 192 kbps CBR MP3 per chapter
  • M4B: a single chaptered file with cover art
  • Take policy: locked-in only, or latest-take fallback
  • Warnings for anything unlocked or unconverted

Generated audio and ACX

ACX does not accept text-to-speech narration. A performance you recorded yourself and converted with speech-to-speech is still your performance, and StellAurator keeps the original recording alongside every converted take.

Every exported file that holds generated narration is marked as AI-generated, and the export dialog says how many of the lines about to go out are generated before you export.

Check every chapter before delivery

Hear the chapter exactly as it will export

Proof listen plays one chapter of one version exactly as the export will write it, and each line you hear gets a tick.

When every line has been heard, Sign off chapter appears. Change a line afterward and only that line goes stale, so you always know what still needs a listen.

  • A heard tick per line, up to 1.5× speed
  • Sign-off per chapter, per version

Flag a line as a pickup

One button on every line, or the F key while anything is playing, marks a take as a pickup without stopping playback.

Everything flagged lands in Pickups. From there: regenerate them, record them, or send them to the actor who read them.

  • Flag from the line, from Review Mode, or with F mid-playback
  • Regenerate flagged · Record flagged · Send pickups

Questions

Questions before you start.

Do I need ElevenLabs or another paid voice service?

No. StellAurator is a recording studio. Voice generation and speech-to-speech conversion are optional, and twenty-six voices come with it and work offline. A paid voice service is needed only for that service’s own voices or for converting your performance into a character’s voice, and it bills you directly. Get an ElevenLabs account if you want one. StellAurator is an independent affiliate of ElevenLabs and is paid a commission if you subscribe after following this link. You can also go to elevenlabs.io directly.

What happens after the seven days?

Nothing is charged: we never asked for a card. The studio becomes read-only. Every book still opens and plays and your recordings are still there. Subscribe when you want to carry on recording, editing and exporting.

Do I lose my projects if I cancel or stop paying?

No. Your projects, recordings and exports are files on your own computer and nothing is ever deleted. If you have ever paid for a plan, export stays unlocked for the work you made.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, in two clicks on your account page. Your plan stays active to the end of the current billing period.

Can I sell the audiobooks I make?

Yes. What you make is yours, and the export is industry standard: ACX chapter MP3s and a chaptered M4B. You are responsible for the rights to your text and to any real person’s voice you clone. Exports that contain generated narration are marked as AI-generated in the file, and ACX does not accept text-to-speech narration.

Does StellAurator upload my manuscript?

No. It stays on your computer. Text is sent only to the voice and AI services you choose to connect, under your own account with them.

How many computers can I use it on?

Three, for one person. The app lists them under Settings, Account, and you can remove one to make room for another. Link them and you can send a book from one to another, keep a book in step, and open your main computer’s library from the second one while the first is on.

Is the installer signed?

Yes. Windows shows the publisher when you run it.

Is it Windows only?

Yes: 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
