Questions

Does StellAurator clean up my recordings?

Yes. While you record, each recording is checked for clicks and pops, for breaths, for hum and for background noise, and a fix for each one is listed under it. Review plays the recording before and after. Finish does the same for a whole chapter, then measures it against ACX’s requirements and exports it. Every fix has an undo, and the original recording is never changed.

Does passing StellAurator’s ACX check mean Audible will accept my book?

No. The check measures each chapter against ACX’s published technical requirements for loudness, peaks, noise floor and file format. ACX also judges the performance and the rights, and it does not accept computer-generated narration.

Can StellAurator split a long recording into lines?

Yes. Record a whole chapter in one session, or import a recording made in another program. It is divided into your lines by the words you read, on your own computer, and nothing is sent. After a false start, record the line again and choose which take to keep.

Can I work with several actors in StellAurator?

Yes, as many as the book needs. Each actor receives an invitation link. Their copy of StellAurator is free and shows only the lines you assign to them, with your direction and the preceding line as a cue. Their recordings come back onto their lines in your book, where you accept each one or mark it for another take.

Can my own reading become the characters’ voices in StellAurator?

Yes. Record every part in your own voice and connect a voice conversion service. Each line is sent for conversion and the converted take is stored beside your original. Your timing and pauses are kept. The exported file is marked as a speech-to-speech modified human performance. Each store decides what it accepts.

Do I lose my StellAurator projects if I cancel or stop paying?

No. Your books and your exported files are on your own computer, and nothing is deleted. If you have ever paid for a plan, export of the work you made stays open.

Do I need a paid voice service to use StellAurator?

No. Twenty-six voices are included and work offline. A paid voice service adds its own voices and voice conversion, and it bills you directly. The voices and services, in full.

Can I sell the audiobooks I make with StellAurator?

Yes. What you make is yours. StellAurator exports in the format Audible’s ACX requires and measures each chapter against ACX’s technical requirements first. The rights to your text, and to any real person’s voice you use, are yours to hold. Files with computer-generated narration are marked as such.

How many computers can I use StellAurator on?

Three computers, for one person. They are listed in the Account settings, and you remove one there to make room for another.
