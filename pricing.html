Renews until you cancel

Plans renew automatically each month or year until you cancel. Cancel anytime from your account, in two clicks; your plan stays active to the end of the current billing period. Trial, subscription and billing terms.

Voice usage is separate

Your subscription covers StellAurator. Cloud voice generation and other paid provider services are billed through your connected accounts. The built-in voices and the Windows voices require no provider account. Get an ElevenLabs account if you want one. StellAurator is an independent affiliate of ElevenLabs and is paid a commission if you subscribe after following this link. You can also go to elevenlabs.io directly.

Generated audio and ACX

ACX does not accept text-to-speech narration. A performance you recorded yourself and converted with speech-to-speech is still your performance, and StellAurator keeps the original recording alongside every converted take. Every exported file that holds generated narration is marked as AI-generated, and the export dialog says how many lines are generated before you export.

Your work stays with you

Your projects, recordings, and exports remain on your computer when a trial or a plan ends. The studio still opens every book and plays it. Subscribe again to resume recording, editing, and creating; if you have ever paid for a plan, export stays unlocked for the work you have made.
