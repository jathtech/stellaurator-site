Questions

Does it clean up my recordings?

Yes. Recordings are analyzed for clicks, pops, plosives, breaths, hum and background noise as you record, and fixes are suggested under each one. Review plays the fixed recording before and after. Finish does the same for a whole chapter, checks it against ACX’s requirements, and exports it. Every fix can be undone, and the original recording is never modified.

Does passing the ACX check mean Audible will accept my book?

No. The check measures each chapter against ACX’s published technical requirements for loudness, peaks, noise floor and file format. Acceptance is ACX’s decision, which also covers the performance and the rights. ACX does not accept computer-generated narration.

Can it split a long recording into lines?

Yes. Record a whole chapter in one session, or import a recording made elsewhere, and it is split into your lines at the pauses, on your own computer. Splitting by the words, so that a reread sentence is matched to its line by what was said, requires a transcription service connected under your own account. After a false start, you choose which reading to keep.

Can I work with several actors?

Yes, as many as the book needs. Each actor receives an invitation link. Their copy of StellAurator is free and shows only the lines you assign to them, with your direction and the preceding line as a cue. Their recordings are returned to you and placed on their lines in your book, where you review, accept, or mark them for another take.

Can my own reading become the characters’ voices?

Yes. Record every part in your own voice and connect a voice conversion service. Each line is sent for conversion and the converted take is stored beside your original. Your timing and pauses are kept. Such a file is marked as a speech-to-speech modified human performance, and whether a store accepts it is the store’s decision.

Do I lose my projects if I cancel or stop paying?

No. Your books, recordings and exported files are stored on your own computer, and nothing is deleted. If you have ever paid for a plan, export of the work you made remains available, so a finished audiobook never requires an ongoing subscription.

How many computers can I use it on?

Three computers, for one person. They are listed under Settings, Account, where one can be removed to make room for another.
